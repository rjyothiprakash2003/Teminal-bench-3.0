Servicing desk procedure, drawdown and schedule issue.

This paper says how the desk works. It settles nothing about the schedule itself.
Servicing note SN-208 governs that, and it is followed wherever the two could
be read differently.

When a facility completes, the relationship manager files the facility papers
as a plain text file and sends it to the servicing desk with the drawdown
advice. The desk runs the builder against that file, the basis table and the
path the schedule is to be filed at, in that order, and posts the schedule it
writes to the borrower and to the ledger.

The basis table is kept once, in the data directory, and is reissued by the
desk head whenever a basis is added or a label is retired. Labels are not
reused. The desk has more than one copy of the table in circulation from
different issues, and the builder is expected to work from whichever copy it is
given rather than from any label it might remember.

Where a rate change is agreed during a term, the manager adds it to the
facility papers and the desk reissues the schedule from the whole file rather
than working out the difference by hand.

Settled facilities are kept in the settled directory. A facility is moved there
when the term has run and the borrower has confirmed the figures. Each folder
holds the papers as they stood and the schedule as it was issued. Where the
servicing note leaves a rule to those schedules, the desk recovers it from them.
